Electronic medical record

Client-sponsored healthcare system for an organization serving 26 hospital locations. REST APIs, Firebase, Kotlin patient services, Java staff endpoints, and HIPAA-focused testing — built to retire spreadsheet workflows.

What it is

A five-person engagement: document the architecture, implement patient and staff services, prove backups match the live store, and walk the sponsor up to administrative ownership. Dietary and patient workflow tracking moved off spreadsheets into structured records. The team measured about 60% improvement in record-processing efficiency.

The year ran in phases: August planning and a proof of concept, September–October for the first front end, the rest of 2021 on the client full stack, January–February on staff services, March–April wiring those two sides, then April–May on securing (HIPAA was reviewed throughout, and drilled hard in April) and a May handoff. Documentation and security work sat beside every phase. There is no public git history for this tree.
