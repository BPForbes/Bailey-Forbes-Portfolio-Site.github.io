At the table

Euros when I want an engine to build, trick-taking when the conversation should stay in the round, and press-your-luck when I want the draw I probably should not take. I was at Gen Con in Indianapolis this year, over the turn of July into August.

  • Unique-deck card game

    KeyForge

    Every deck is generated once and never printed again — no deckbuilding, no hunting singles. Three houses per deck, one house per turn, and you race to forge three keys. I have played since launch in November 2018.

  • Trick-taking · 4 players

    Euchre

    Twenty-four cards, two partnerships, and a trump suit where the two jacks outrank everything. Fast enough that the table keeps talking. It is close to a state pastime in Indiana.

  • Euro · engine building

    Puerto Rico

    Each round you pick a role — settler, builder, craftsman, captain — and everyone takes that action, but you get the bonus. The whole game is choosing the thing that helps you more than it helps them.

  • Deck-builder · push your luck

    Clank!

    A dungeon crawl on top of a deck-builder. Every noisy card drops a cube in the bag, the dragon draws from that bag, and going one room deeper is always tempting. You still have to get back out.

  • Route building · the gateway

    Ticket to Ride

    Collect coloured train cards, claim routes, quietly complete the destination tickets in your hand. Simple enough to teach in five minutes and still the one I reach for with a new table.
